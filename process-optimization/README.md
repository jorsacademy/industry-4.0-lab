# Process Optimization

Projects in this domain connect physically meaningful manufacturing responses to process-setting decisions. Models are evaluated at the physical run/condition level, and optimization is restricted to experimentally supported operating regions unless a stronger extrapolation argument is available.

## Projects

- [CNC Turning — Surface Integrity, Cutting Forces & Process Trade-offs](cnc-turning-quality-force/) — real experimental benchmark.
- [Welding Process Parameter Optimization](welding-process-parameter-optimization/) — physics-informed synthetic prototype.
- [Sheet-Metal Stamping Process Optimization](sheet-metal-stamping-process-optimization/) — physics-informed synthetic prototype.
- [Laser Cutting Process Optimization](laser-cutting-process-optimization/) — physics-informed synthetic prototype.
- [Heat-Treatment Profile Optimization](heat-treatment-profile-optimization/) — physics-informed synthetic prototype.
- [Grinding Process Adaptive Control](grinding-process-adaptive-control/) — physics-informed synthetic adaptive-control prototype.
- [Machining Coolant Flow Optimization](machining-coolant-flow-optimization/) — physics-informed synthetic prototype.

Synthetic prototypes demonstrate software architecture, surrogate modeling and constrained decision layers. They are deliberately separated from the real-data benchmark and must be calibrated before any industrial recommendation is made.
